So, I wrote this blog post to show the different types of dining tables that are best for small spaces. You will also find tips for choosing and styling the perfect dining table for your home. So what dining table is best for a small space? There are a few types of tables that are suitable for small spaces. They are round dining tables, extendable tables, drop-leaf tables, bar height tables, and wall-mounted tables. We’ll look at them in detail below. We’ll also look at the various things to consider when choosing a dining table for small spaces.

## Different Types of Dining Tables for Small Spaces

There are many types of dining tables. But we are interested in the ones that are practical for small spaces. Let’s look at each one.

### Round Dining Table

Round dining tables are a great choice for small dining rooms or kitchens. They take up less room compared to the traditional rectangle tables. These tables come with a circular top usually supported by four legs. And, they can seat about four to six people depending on the size of the table.

You will find them made with various materials such as wood, metal, or glass. The tops come fixed while others come with leaves to extend for more seating capacity. Some styles also come in different heights like counter height or bar height.

Round dining tables promote conversation and a sense of intimacy. They are also safe for small children as there are no sharp edges. All in all, they work great for small spaces.

### Extendable Table

Extendable dining tables are also known as expandable dining tables. You can extend these tables to accommodate more people, and stay compact when not in use. This makes it an ideal choice for small dining rooms or kitchens. Usually, the smaller tables can seat four people. Whereas, the larger ones can seat eight or even more.

They come in various designs and styles, and they all have a mechanism that makes them expand in size. The most common feature among them is that you can add or remove leaves to expand or shrink the table’s size. You will also find them made of wood, metal, or glass. And they come in various styles, from traditional to modern.

There are a few types of extendable dining tables:

-   Self-storing leaf tables: These types of tables hold the leaves within them. This is so that you won’t need extra space to put the leaves away when not in use.
-   Butterfly leaf tables: These come with leaves stored under the top. You can extend the table by pulling out the leaves or folding them in to make it smaller.
-   Skovby leaf system: Extendable dining tables using this system has a leaf under the top. You expand these types of tables by pulling out the leaf.
-   Gateleg tables: These types of tables have legs that fold out to support the extended leaves.

### Drop-Leaf Table

Drop-leaf dining tables come with hinged leaves on one or both sides of the table. You can fold these leaves to make the table smaller when not in use. By design, these are tables made for small spaces.

They come in various sizes, styles, and designs. As with most dining tables, they too come in wood, metal, and glass finishes. You will also find them in traditional and modern designs. Some of them come round, while others are square or rectangle. Besides, they also come freestanding or wall-mounted, which is ideal for small spaces.

There are more or less two types of drop-leaf tables. They are single drop-leaf and double drop-leaf tables. The single drop-leaf table that comes with a single hinged leaf only on one side of the table. And, the double drop-leaf table has two hinged leaves on either side of the table.

Some of them come with extra features such as storage options underneath the table top or on the sides. This gives you more incentive to use for a small dining room or kitchen. The added storage space is definitely a plus point.

### Bar Height Table

Bar-height dining tables are a great option for small spaces. They are taller than standard dining tables, typically around 42 inches in height. This means they take up less floor space and can accommodate more seating in a smaller area. Plus, they can double as a counter or workspace, which is perfect for small apartments or studios where every piece of furniture needs to serve multiple purposes.

Another advantage of bar-height dining tables is that they can make a small space feel bigger. By drawing the eye upward, they create the illusion of more height and open space. This can be especially helpful in a small room.

Bar-height dining tables come in a variety of materials, styles and designs. They can be made of wood, metal, glass and many other materials. They can also have different shapes such as round, square, or rectangular. This allows you to find the perfect one that fits your personal style and decor.

In summary, bar-height dining tables are a great option for small spaces. They take up less floor space, can double as a counter or workspace, can make a small space feel bigger and come in a variety of materials, styles and designs. They are a versatile option that can accommodate various needs and personal styles.

### Wall-Mounted Table

Wall-mounted dining tables are a unique and innovative solution for small spaces. They are tables that come mounted onto a wall, rather than taking up valuable floor space. This makes them perfect for small apartments, studios, or  homes with limited space.

The design is sleek and modern, and they come in a variety of finishes, so you can find one that matches your decor. They usually have a small footprint and can seat a small group of people.

The best thing about wall-mounted dining tables is that they are extremely versatile. When not in use, you can fold the table against the wall freeing up valuable space. And when you need to use it, you  simply unfold it and enjoy a meal with your family or friends. This means you can have a dining area when you need it, without it taking up space when you don’t.

Another great thing about wall-mounted dining tables is that they are easy to clean and maintain. With the table mounted on the wall, there is less chance of crumbs or spills getting stuck in hard-to-reach places.

In conclusion, wall-mounted dining tables are a great option for small spaces. They are a great solution if you are looking for a dining table that fits a small dining room or kitchen. They are also easy to clean and maintain which make them a great addition to any small space.

## What to Look for When Choosing a Dining Table for a Small Space

When choosing a dining table for a small room, there are a few factors to consider. Start with the size and shape. A round or square table is better for small spaces as opposed to rectangular tables.

Also, look for space-saving features such as extra storage space, or extendable leaves. You can look for foldable or extendable leaves as they are a great way to preserve space when not used. Furthermore, extendable tables can add functionality to optimize space. For example, you can add leaves to entertain more guests and convert them to a bar table.

Next, consider the style and finish. Clear acrylic or glass tables can make a small space feel larger, while wooden tables give a warm feeling. Also, see how the table complements the aesthetic of your dining space. Sleek and modern styles can give a touch of elegance. And rustic or farmhouse styles create a cozy atmosphere.

## How to Style a Small Dining Table

There are a few important considerations to make while decorating a dining table. Consider the look you want to achieve first. Do you prefer a sleek, contemporary appearance or something more conventional? This will assist you in selecting the appropriate table and matching accessories.

Next, take your table’s size and form into account. You should pick a table that is portable and compact if your room is limited. Yet, if your eating space is huge, you might want to choose a table that can accommodate a lot of people.

Consider using color, texture, and lighting to decorate your table. To add color and texture, use placemats, table runners, candles, or flowers. Make sure there is adequate lighting above your table. Or, use table lights to make it the center of attention in your dining room.

Have fun and don’t be afraid to try out new trends and fashions. Dining tables are a wonderful way to showcase your style and give your home a special atmosphere. So feel free to mash up various looks and have fun!

## Make Your Dining Space Feel Larger

When it comes to small dining spaces, it can be a challenge to make them feel larger and more inviting. But don’t worry, there are a few simple tricks you can use to make the most of your space!

One of the best ways to create the illusion of more space is to keep things minimal and uncluttered. This means avoiding overcrowding the room with too many furniture pieces. Instead, opt for a few key pieces that are functional and versatile. A dining table that can be folded or extended, for example, is a great choice for a small space.

Another great tip for making a small dining space feel larger is to play with light and color. A room that is well-lit and painted in light neutral colors will feel much larger and more open. Additionally, using mirrors can also make the room feel larger. This is because reflecting light creates the illusion of more space.

Finally, it’s important to think about the layout of your dining space. An open floor plan, where the dining area flows into the living room, can make a small space feel much larger. Opting for a smaller dining table and using benches instead of chairs too can help save space.

Being mindful of the furniture, layout, and decor you choose. By following these tips you can make a small dining area feel open, inviting, and larger than it actually is. Happy decorating!

In conclusion, finding the perfect dining table for a small space can be a daunting task, but it doesn’t have to be. By considering factors such as size, shape, and expandability, you can narrow down your options and find a table that not only fits your space, but also enhances it. Additionally, don’t be afraid to get creative with styling and decor to make your dining area feel larger. Remember, a little bit of planning and thought can go a long way in making the most out of your small space. Happy hunting!
